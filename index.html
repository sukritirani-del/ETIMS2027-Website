The host institute

About NSIT

Established in 2007, Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology (NSIT) is located at Amhara, Bihta, approximately 30 km from Patna, Bihar. The institute was established with the approval of AICTE and the Department of Science and Technology, Government of Bihar.

Its campus includes laboratories, workshops, a digital library, auditorium, hostels and sports facilities.

B.Tech programmes are affiliated with Bihar Engineering University, diploma programmes with the State Board of Technical Education, and management programmes with Aryabhatta Knowledge University.

Call for papers

Explore the research tracks

ETIMS 2027 invites original, unpublished research papers across eight tracks. Related multidisciplinary contributions are also welcome.

T1

Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence

AI, machine learning and deep learning; data science; cloud and high-performance computing; cybersecurity and blockchain; computer vision and NLP; software engineering.

T2

Civil Engineering and Infrastructure

Structural, geotechnical, transportation and railway engineering; pavements; water resources and environmental engineering; construction management; advanced construction materials; geospatial technologies.

T3

Electrical Engineering

Renewable energy; power systems and smart grids; power electronics and drives; electrical machines; energy storage; electric vehicles; energy management.

T4

Mechanical and Manufacturing Engineering

Advanced and additive manufacturing; Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing; robotics and automation; thermal and fluid engineering; automobile engineering; mechanical design and computational mechanics.

T5

Electronics and Communication Engineering

Wireless and 5G/6G communication; IoT and embedded systems; VLSI; signal and image processing; antennas and microwave engineering; sensors; optical communication.

T6

Applied Science

Applied and condensed-matter physics; photonics and thin films; nanomaterials and functional materials; electrochemistry and energy-storage materials; catalysis, polymers and green chemistry; environmental and analytical chemistry; mathematical modelling, numerical methods, optimization, statistics and cryptography.

T7

Management

Technology and innovation management; operations and supply-chain management; project and construction management; entrepreneurship and start-up ecosystems; business analytics and decision sciences; human resource and organisational management; financial management and FinTech; sustainable business and ESG practices.

T8

Humanities and Library Science

English language, literature and linguistics; professional and technical communication; ethics, society and culture; digital humanities; library and information science; digital libraries and knowledge management; scholarly communication and research metrics; information literacy.
